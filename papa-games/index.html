Bobcat Bowl

You are the Bobcats! Beat the Coyotes. 🏈

OFFENSE: call a play (Quick pass, Deep pass, Run or QB run), read the coverage tip, then press HIKE (Space). Pass = click a receiver (or press 1 2 3), green ring = open! Running: ↑ ↓ steer, → boost, Space juke.
DEFENSE: pick Man, Zone or Blitz, then steer your dot with the arrows, Shift = boost, Space = switch to the player near the ball (you have to press it!). Tackle the ball carrier!
REF MODE: throw the flag (Space) when you see a foul: a player over the blue line before the snap, a player jumping early, a yellow line between two players, or a hit on the QB.
After a touchdown, kick the extra point (+1) or GO FOR 2!
T = timeout (stops the clock). Watch out for 🚩 penalties! After you score you can try an ONSIDE kick. P pauses.
