🎵 How to use it

  1. Pick a song on the left, then press ▶ Play (or the Space bar).
  2. Make your own notes: click a square in the big grid. Click it again to remove it. Drag sideways to make a long note.
  3. Play live: press keys on your keyboard (Z X A S D F G H J K L) or click the piano at the bottom.
  4. Change the sound: pick a part (like Part 1), then pick an instrument, like 🎸 Electric Guitar.
  5. Record: press ● Rec and play the keys along with the beat. Your notes go into the grid!

Tip: use BPM +/− to go faster or slower. 🔊 mutes a part and ✕ clears it.
